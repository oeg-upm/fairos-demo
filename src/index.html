ABOUT
FAIROs

        
FAIROs evaluates FAIRness of Research Objects and exposes assessment results using the FAIR Testing Resource (FTR) model.

      

      

        
About FAIROs

        
FAIROs is a FAIR assessment service designed to evaluate Research Objects and FAIR Digital Objects. It applies a configurable set of FAIR tests and combines their results using an assessment algorithm to produce an overall score. The service can assess the Research Object together with its constituent resources and represents the resulting assessment in a machine-actionable form.
